About Volantio

Building the system that recovers
what airlines leave behind

Volantio was founded in 2014 on a simple belief — airlines could drive higher profit from passengers already on their planes, without adding acquisition cost. Vector is the demand reallocation platform that delivers on that promise today: governed execution, with safety and accuracy when passengers are affected.

Our mission

Demand reallocation airlines can trust at passenger scale

We help carriers close the gap between insight and compliant action in the PSS and passenger channels. Vector keeps humans in charge while shortening the window from signal to execution — so revenue, operations, and customer teams share one system of record.

Our story

A decade building the right system

2014

The founding insight

Azim Barodawala and Fenn Bailey founded Volantio in Atlanta with one conviction: passenger flexibility is an untapped airline asset. The first products turned that flexibility into voluntary moves and revenue recovery — and set the foundation for everything that followed.

The build

Years of airline-grade engineering

Direct integrations with leading PSS providers. AI prediction models refined on real booking data. Passenger engagement strategies optimised for the highest acceptance rates. A decade of iteration until the system could operate reliably, at scale, across live airline operations.

Today

Vector

Vector is the agent-based execution layer that carries Volantio’s decade of airline operations learning: discovery and scoring on top of your data, offers and PSS moves inside policies you define, and evidence for every passenger-affecting decision. It is the evolution of our platform — not a generic AI bolt-on.
