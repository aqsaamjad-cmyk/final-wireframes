How we support you

Implementation speed, ongoing care

Vector is built to reach production in weeks-first time horizons — not multi-year programmes — using the integrations and governance paths airlines already trust. Exact duration depends on scenario scope, PSS path complexity, and how many channels you light up first.

Implementation phases

Every engagement is led with a shared workbook so revenue, operations, technology, and customer stakeholders see the same checkpoints.

  1. Discover & scope — scenarios, policy boundaries, channel inventory, success measures.
  2. Configure & integrate — rules, eligibility, test harnesses, non-prod PSS paths.
  3. Pilot & harden — limited production lanes, operational observation, refinement of confirm queues.
  4. Scale & expand — widen markets or agents with the same audit and monitoring spine.

Who is in the room

Volantio solution architects pair with your integration owners; commercial leads align on offer strategy; ops validates playbooks; security reviews controls using artifacts summarized on Security & Trust.

Customer care & escalation

After go-live, named customer leads stay engaged for configuration tweaks, monitoring reviews, and incident coordination. Severity-based escalation reaches engineering when live execution or integrations are impacted.

Volantio aligns release windows with your change calendars so upgrades do not collide with peak operational periods you flag in advance.

Training & adoption

Enablement focuses on how analysts interpret recommendations, how ops uses pause and override, and how audit teams pull evidence — keeping day-two operations as smooth as launch week.
