Vector overview

Vector gives airlines a safe execution layer to turn demand reallocation opportunities into action.

Innovating for the future. The platform works across three steps:

Find the opportunity Vector reasons across airline data, signals, customer behavior, and system context to surface where demand can be reallocated.

Shape the action Teams can ask Vector to define the right agent, rules, eligibility, offer logic, timing, and customer experience.

Execute with control Vector acts through existing airline systems, within airline-defined rules, with every action visible and auditable.

Vector agents

Agents are how the platform comes to life.

Agents turn overbooking pressure, disruptions, and shifting demand into repeatable, governed actions—wired to eligibility, timing, and offers your airline owns.

You start with patterns that already ship today, then extend the same rules-and-execution backbone as priorities change—without chaining brittle point fixes.
