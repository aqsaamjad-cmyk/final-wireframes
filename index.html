Why teams trust Vector

AI with built-in safety guardrails.

Vector does not hand AI the keys to critical systems. It sits between intelligence and action as a secure, controlled layer: recommendations and next steps route through workflows your airline already trusts—not open-ended automation into core passenger or revenue platforms. Every governed action stays rule constrained, visible, and auditable.

You stay in charge

Vector separates analysis from fulfillment: models can surface what matters, while changes still move through integrations, checkpoints, and teams your airline already controls—rather than unattended access to passenger or operational systems.

Clear audit trail

What was considered, approved, communicated, and logged reads as one story—without parallel chats, spreadsheets, or ad hoc channels that obscure who did what, and why.

Rules you define

Eligibility, timing, channels, and risk posture stay defined by each carrier—Vector only orchestrates governed actions inside those boundaries, so automation never outruns policy.
