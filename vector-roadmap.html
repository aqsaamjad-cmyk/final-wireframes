Roadmap

More agents, more use cases

The same execution layer that supports Arbitrage, Oversell, and Signals is designed to expand — including irregular operations (IROPs) and new demand reallocation plays your airline defines.

This page describes direction only. Timelines and feature scope are negotiated per carrier; nothing here is a contractual commitment or availability guarantee.

In market today

Core demand reallocation agents

Arbitrage, Oversell, and Signals share policy, audit, and integration patterns on Vector. Carriers typically start with one or two high-value scenarios, then widen eligibility and channels on the same foundation.

Exploring with partners

IROPs and disruption-at-scale

Irregular operations introduce compressed decision windows, crew constraints, and passenger comms volume. Volantio works with airline operations and technology teams on how Vector’s governed execution model extends to those moments — without bypassing the controls that make live PSS integration acceptable day to day.

  • ·Faster triage of who can be moved voluntarily when the network reshapes
  • ·Shared audit narrative across commercial and ops for downstream review
  • ·Alignment with your existing crisis playbooks and escalation trees

How prioritization works

Joint backlog, airline-shaped

Roadmap ordering weighs regulatory environment, PSS and partner dependencies, and where your network sees the next threshold of value. Product, engineering, and customer leads meet carriers on a regular rhythm so effort maps to operational reality — not a generic release calendar.
