Vector · How It Works

From signal to governed execution

Vector connects discovery, analysis, and execution for demand reallocation — with rules your airline defines, visibility your teams require, and accuracy shaped by a decade in the problem.

Discovery

Continuous alignment with airline data

Vector reasons across PSS, distribution and subscriber signals, and operational context — not a single static rules sheet.

Analysis

Recommendations with clear impact

Ranked suggestions (Alerts, Optimize, Opportunities) help teams prioritize where demand reallocation will matter most — before the window closes.

Execution

Rules engine + proven PSS paths

Actions run through existing airline systems. Human confirm steps, audit trails, and boundaries you set keep speed paired with safety and accuracy.

When teams use Vector

Rhythms of the operating day

Ahead of departure, commercial and operations teams watch for load imbalance, creeping oversell, or disruption-driven pressure. Vector surfaces reallocation opportunities while there is still time to coordinate offers and PSS changes cleanly.

During campaigns or network stress, analysts use Vector to align inventory, flexibility products, and eligibility rules — so execution does not depend on one-off spreadsheets or manual queues.

After significant events, the same audit trail that supports live execution helps revenue assurance and compliance teams reconstruct what was offered, accepted, and written to the record.

Governance

Humans stay in charge

Vector is not autonomous control of your PSS. Your airline sets the boundary conditions: which agents run, for which markets or flights, at what thresholds, and with which confirmation steps.

  • 01Policy and eligibility travel with every recommendation so reviewers see why an action is allowed before it is sent.
  • 02Optional confirm queues let you require a human accept for high-impact moves or sensitive periods.
  • 03Pause, override, and rollback remain available so operations can respond faster than any ticket queue when reality changes.
  • 04Evidence by design: signal, scoring rationale, and system writes are logged for the teams who sign off on passenger-affecting change.

What “good” looks like

Policy you can defend, outcomes you can measure

Every airline defines “good” slightly differently. Vector is built to operationalize that definition — not replace it.

Policy clarity

Eligibility, compensation, channel treatment, and escalation paths are explicit in configuration so commercial, ops, and legal stakeholders share one source of truth.

Measurement

Teams track uptake, revenue-effect proxies, disruption cost avoided, and passenger-contact outcomes — with segmentations you care about (hub, route group, cabin, etc.). Exact KPI packs are finalized during implementation.

Agents

Same layer, different pressure points

Agents package how Vector discovers, explains, and executes for a class of problem. They share telemetry, policy, and audit patterns — so adding scope does not mean another disconnected toolchain.

  • Arbitrage — shift flexible demand from peak departures to alternatives where the network benefits.
  • Oversell — recover voluntary moves and protect the gate before involuntary denial becomes likely.
  • Signals — find travelers likely to accept change and steer demand toward inventory that needs filling.
