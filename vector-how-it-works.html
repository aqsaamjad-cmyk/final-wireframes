The execution flow

Three stages. Every departure. Every agent.

The same loop runs across every agent and every departure window.

01

Detect

Reads PSS load, passenger eligibility, fare rules, and operational signals — surfacing departures where demand reallocation is feasible before the window narrows.

02

Decide

Every candidate is evaluated against your airline's rules — fare class, load thresholds, compensation caps, blackout windows. Only what qualifies proceeds. The rationale travels with every recommendation.

03

Execute

Approved actions write through your existing PSS and passenger channels. Every action closes with a full audit record — signal, rationale, PSS write, and outcome.

Rules engine

Your policy travels with every action.

Vector does not decide what is allowed — your airline does. The rules engine enforces fare class restrictions, load thresholds, passenger eligibility criteria, compensation caps, and blackout windows before any action is queued.

Rules are configured once and applied continuously. Teams do not need to recheck policy on each recommendation — the engine has already eliminated anything that does not qualify.

PSS connectivity

Works inside the systems your airline already runs.

Vector connects to your existing PSS and passenger channels — no rip-and-replace, no parallel booking stack. Integrations are proven across major global carriers and configured during implementation alongside your IT and ops teams.

Writes through your existing PSS

Vector executes actions through your existing PSS paths — no new infrastructure, no parallel booking layer. Your operational systems stay unchanged.

Existing channels

Passenger outreach uses your email, app, and contact center infrastructure — not a new channel layer.

Amadeus · Sabre · Navitaire

Connection patterns are established, not experimental. Timelines and requirements are defined during scoping.

Guardrails

Built-in guardrails your team controls.

Automation without oversight is not a product airlines can trust. Vector is built around the assumption that human teams need to stay in control at every step.

01

Policy & eligibility visible before action

Every recommendation shows which rules allowed it — so reviewers approve with context, not faith.

02

Approval queues for high-impact actions

Require a human approval step for sensitive periods, high-value passengers, or any market your team designates.

03

Pause, override, and rollback

Stop any agent instantly. Override individual decisions. Roll back a PSS write before it propagates downstream.

04

Evidence by design

Signal, scoring rationale, and system writes are logged immutably — for the teams who sign off on passenger-affecting change.

When teams use it

Demand reallocation has rhythms. Vector fits them.

Ahead of departure

Commercial and operations teams watch for load imbalance, creeping oversell, or disruption-driven pressure. Vector surfaces reallocation opportunities while there is still time to coordinate offers and PSS changes cleanly.

During revenue campaigns or network disruption

Analysts use Vector to align inventory, flexibility products, and eligibility rules across revenue campaigns, schedule changes, or network disruption events — so execution does not depend on one-off spreadsheets or manual queues.

After significant events

The same audit trail that supports live execution helps revenue assurance and compliance teams reconstruct what was offered, accepted, and written to the record.
