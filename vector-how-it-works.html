PSS Connectivity

Vector runs alongside your existing airline systems. Not instead of them.

Vector is designed to work within existing airline infrastructure — connecting with PSS environments, revenue systems, and operational workflows without requiring rip-and-replace deployments.

The platform helps airlines operationalize demand reallocation while maintaining continuity across existing systems and processes.

Vector sits between PSS, revenue, and operational systems — the Vector coordination layer — and channels your teams already use, integrating without replacing the core stack.

Operational Guardrails

Built-in guardrails your team controls

Airlines need visibility and control over how operational workflows are coordinated and executed.

Vector is designed to keep teams involved at every stage — with approvals, oversight, auditability, and operational safeguards built directly into the workflow layer.
