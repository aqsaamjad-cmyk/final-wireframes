Vector · Signals

Signals

Find flexible demand and guide it toward inventory that needs filling — with relevance your revenue teams can explain and passengers experience as helpful, not noisy.

The problem

Not every empty seat needs a sale; some need the right traveler

Blanket promotions blunt brand and margin. What works better is knowing which booked travelers are plausibly flexible, on which flows, and whether moving them unlocks value for the network without breaking commitments elsewhere.

Signals exists to target demand stimulation and reshaping so commercial teams act with precision instead of volume.

How Vector surfaces it

Flexible intent, bounded by policy

Signals blends itinerary context, product attachment, historical flexibility where available, and guardrails you define (markets, cabins, time-before-departure) to produce a ranked view of who might accept a change that helps the network.

Recommendations land beside the Alerts and Opportunities your analysts already use — so prioritization stays in one place.

Compliant execution

Channel-native outreach, measured results

Execution respects customer preferences and regulatory constraints: frequency caps, consent where required, and clear presentation of alternatives. Acceptances write back through the same PSS paths as other Vector agents, preserving a single audit narrative.

That is how teams scale accuracy: fewer mistargeted offers, clearer uplift attribution, and the ability to prove what was shown to whom.

Who cares

Revenue management and marketing, same truth

Commercial

Fill weaker departures and protect high-value flows without carpet-bombing fares or loyalty balances.

Customer

Passengers who can move get timely, relevant options; those who need stability are left alone.
