Vector · Arbitrage

Arbitrage

Move demand from peak departures to alternatives where your network has room — recovering seat value before the door closes.

The problem

Heat concentrates where you cannot board everyone profitably

Some flights attract more demand than you can carry at the fare mix you sold. The classic response — discounting backward or hoping for no-shows — burns yield or pushes risk to the gate. Meanwhile other legs in the same market day still have seats you would rather fill.

The opportunity is demand reallocation: inviting flexible travelers to move to a flight that works better for the network, with an offer they can accept in your channels.

How Vector surfaces it

Ranked moves, not spreadsheets

Arbitrage continuously aligns load, eligibility, and business rules to flag departures where shifting voluntary demand is both feasible and valuable. Teams see suggested moves with context: who might move, to which alternative, and why the suggestion survives policy checks.

Because Vector shares the same discovery fabric as other agents, you are not maintaining parallel lists of “special projects” per corridor.

Compliant execution

Offers and PSS paths you already trust

Accepted changes execute through your passenger channels and booking stack with the same controls as the rest of Vector: confirmation where you require it, immutable logging of what was promised, and boundaries for compensation or re-accommodation.

Speed never comes at the expense of safety and accuracy — reviewers can pause a lane, override a flight, or walk the audit trail when regulators or partners ask questions.

Who cares

Commercial and network teams, aligned

Commercial

Protect revenue per seat on constrained departures while harvesting fills where load factors still have headroom.

Operations

Reduce last-minute juggling at the hub; see demand shifts land in the record with time to staff and communicate.
